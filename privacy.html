Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how RaffleWheel treats your data seamlessly behind the scenes.

Last Updated: June 2, 2026

Welcome to RaffleWheel ("we", "our", or "us"). We run the free online wheel spinner application found at https://rafflewheel.github.io/. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to data minimalization and your personal information privacy while using our service.

1. No User Accounts & Registration

RaffleWheel is entirely open-access. We do not require account registration, email verification, or social logins to use our services. Because you do not make an account, we do not compile user profiles or capture identities dynamically mapping to our users.

2. Local Storage and Browser State

To deliver automated functionalities (like remembering your customized layouts, customized lists of entries, settings toggles, or custom color palettes), all corresponding details are saved exclusively inside your computer browser using technology known as LocalStorage. This processing cycle stays entirely inside your equipment; your list entries are never synchronized or broadcast into our web infrastructure servers.

3. Analytical Tracking Data

We leverage basic tracking instruments (such as privacy-friendly web analytics tools) to isolate usage data patterns, traffic volumes, and general performance health. The processed insights aggregate variables like device variant metrics, general geo-location zones, browser variations, and interactive exit-entry signals. This metadata is parsed exclusively to debug system errors and refine app components.

4. Third-Party Advertisement Frameworks

To guarantee that our random wheel picker application stays 100% free to use for classrooms, streamers, and organizers, we might support our running overhead costs by showing third-party networks advertisements. These partners may parse non-personally identifiable metrics (such as your structural system type or relative interactive frequencies) via cookie records to optimize ad placements tailored specifically to your browsing habits.

5. External Web Integrations

Our layout components contains operational hyperlinks navigating outward to outside platforms. If you click on an external link, you leave RaffleWheel. We maintain zero command or validation control regarding external platform properties, and we suggest examining their specific custom privacy frameworks before interacting with them.

6. Children's Informational Protections

Our classroom student selection tools are intentionally designed safe for teacher execution inside classroom environments. Because we do not harvest or aggregate structural database entries remotely on centralized server locations, we do not knowingly parse, record, or track private identifying keys originating from children under any legal age bounds.

7. Adjustments to This Frame

We retain operational permissions to modify this privacy blueprint smoothly over time. Any revisions automatically go live upon rendering the updated configuration markup text on this digital canvas. Continued access to our spinner interface acts as validation of our modern active operational frameworks.

8. Contact Inquiries

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your local browser state works, please drop an issue via our formal GitHub hosting repository channels.
